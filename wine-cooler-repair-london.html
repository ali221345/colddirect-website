Wine cooler service London

Wine cooler service London is PPM on dual-zone cabinets: we check both bands, door seals, condenser airflow and set points after a power cut. Servicing a pass display before Saturday dinner stops one zone drifting while the other still looks fine. A service contract for a hotel or off-licence merchandiser puts those checks on a diary. We still run 24/7 wine cooler repair when sparkling is already warm. Planned servicing keeps by-the-glass stock in the band the sommelier printed on the list.

Dual-zone cabinets fail on one zone while the other still looks fine. Glass-door units ice on the evaporator and then flood. Controllers lose their set point after a power cut. Condensers behind a tight bar void block with dust. Door seals on constantly opened pass cabinets perish. We test both zones, check fans, and look at how the unit is ventilated. A wine cooler pushed tight to a plasterboard wall will keep failing in summer.
