Common Gram Fridge Faults We Fix in London

  • Electronic thermostat out of calibration. The Gram screen is confident and wrong. Product sits at eight while the board thinks it is at two. We replace or recalibrate the control and leave a probe note, not a turned-down set point that ices the coil.
  • Door heater open circuit on a glass Gram. The pane sweats, then ices at the frame, and the door sticks. We test the heater tape and the gasket magnet so the leaf closes without a scrape.
  • Oil-logged evaporator after repeated short cycles. A dirty condenser makes the Gram cut in and out. Oil stays in the coil and capacity drops. We clean the condenser, check the charge, and only then consider a compressor if amps are still wrong.
