Common Commercial Appliance Faults We Diagnose & Fix on First Visit

  • Cabinet Warming / High Temperature Alarms: Faulty Dixell, Carel or Eliwell digital controllers, failed NTC/PTC probes, blocked condenser coils, or seized condenser and evaporator fans.
  • Compressor Tripping or Short-Cycling: Failed start relays, run capacitors, thermal overloads, or low refrigerant charge (R290, R404A, R448A, R449A, R134a) diagnosed and re-gassed by REFCOM F-Gas certified engineers.
  • Ice Build-Up & Water Leaks: Open-circuit defrost heater elements, faulty defrost termination stats, blocked condensate drain lines and perished magnetic door gaskets.
