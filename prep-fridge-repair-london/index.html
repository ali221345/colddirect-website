Commercial Fridge Not Cooling / Leaking Water

Clearing blocked condensate drains, replacing seized evaporator fan motors, cleaning grease-choked condensers and recalibrating Dixell/Carel thermostats when cabinets climb above 5°C–8°C.

Walk-In Freezer Temperature High & Icing Up

Diagnosing burnt-out electric defrost heaters, termination klixons, iced evaporator coils, torn magnetic door gaskets and high-pressure condensing unit trips on -18°C to -22°C rooms.
