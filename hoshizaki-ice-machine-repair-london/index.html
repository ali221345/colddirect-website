How a Hoshizaki cuber actually fails

A KM-series machine fills, freezes on a stainless evaporator, then harvests cubes into a bin. London mains water is hard. Scale on the evaporator plate is the weekday classic: long freeze times, thin cubes, then a machine that never harvests. Inlet strainers block. Float valves stick. Purge drains lime up. Water pumps fail. Inlet valves leak and overfill. Those are water-side jobs. The refrigeration side is a dirty air-cooled condenser behind a bar that nobody vacuums, a failed condenser fan, a weak charge after a vibration leak, or a compressor that has been restarted dry after someone “just switched it off and on”.

A bin that is empty at 22:00 with a machine that still sounds busy is often a harvest problem, not an empty gas bottle story. We watch a cycle. We do not guess from the bin. Modular heads on large hotel bins have a different water and drain layout to a small IM under-counter. Tell us which when you call so the van is stocked.

What we do on site

We read the model and serial from the plate, inspect water filters, strainers, the evaporator plate, the condenser, the bin switch and the controller alarms. We descale when scale is the fault and the plate is still serviceable. We replace pumps, valves and fans when they are not. We leak-test if the charge looks light. We will not leave a machine electrically unsafe. We will not keep running a cuber with no water. A dry restart is how you buy a new compressor.

Hygiene matters on ice. A bin with pink slime or a machine that has not been cleaned is a food-safety issue as well as a mechanical one. We will say if the bin needs a proper clean before you sell ice again. That is not optional wording for a hotel still-room.

Before the engineer arrives

Switch the machine off if it is running dry or if water is on the floor. Photograph the model plate. Note whether it is air-cooled or water-cooled. Clear the condenser intake. Do not pour descaler in at random if you do not know the model. Have the water isolator accessible. If the bar is still trading, tell us so we can plan a bin-safe isolation.

Why Hoshizaki machines stop making ice in London

  • Hard mains water scales the evaporator; freeze time stretches until harvest never starts.
  • Bar-side condensers clog with dust and napkins; head pressure rises and the machine trips.
