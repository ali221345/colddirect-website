If the display is warm, you cannot sell the food

We provide both repair and service / maintenance for commercial display cabinets. Display cabinets are the shop window of chilled food. Serve-over counters in a deli or butchers, multideck cases in a supermarket, and glass-door uprights in a convenience store all have one job: hold product in the legal band while customers can still see it. When the air curtain collapses, the night blind is left up, or the evaporator ices, the top shelf cooks and the bottom shelf ices. That is a waste event and an EHO event. Display cabinet repair is therefore a same-day job for most retailers.

Display cabinet service London

Display cabinet service London is the night-fill visit that stores forget until a bay is warm. PPM servicing covers night blinds, honeycomb, evaporator fans, door heaters and probe checks on the top shelf. A service contract for a supermarket aisle or deli serve-over means we log case temperatures for the EHO before they ask. Servicing after a deep clean stops the case being stacked above the load line the next morning. We still repair failed decks 24/7. Planned servicing just keeps more of the aisle selling.
