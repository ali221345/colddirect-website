Do you offer 24/7 commercial refrigeration repair in London?

Yes. Typical emergency attendance is two to four hours across our 25 mile North London radius, including evenings and Sundays when stock is at risk. A warm walk-in on a Saturday night is treated as an emergency, not a Monday ticket.

Is Cold Direct commercial only?

Yes. We repair trade kitchens, shops and hotels. Household fridge-freezers are not the default diary. If you are unsure whether your cabinet is commercial, send a photo of the data plate. Related: commercial fridge repair London.

Do you quote before starting a repair?

Yes. We diagnose on site and quote the repair before we start a long job. If a part must be ordered, we tell you the lead time before you commit. We will not leave a cabinet electrically unsafe or recharge a leaking system and drive away.

How much is a commercial fridge call-out in London?

In 2026 a trade call-out plus diagnosis typically sits around £90 to £150 ex VAT in Greater London. Night and Sunday emergency work is higher. You pay for the visit even if you decline the repair. Bands for labour and parts are on our commercial fridge repair cost guide.

Which brands do you repair?

Foster, Williams, True, Gram, Polar, Adexa, Empire, Liebherr, Hoshizaki, Sub-Zero and most other trade cabinets. Send a photo of the data plate. See the full list on brands.

Are engineers F-Gas certified?

Refrigerant handling on F-Gas systems is done by F-Gas competent engineers. Registration numbers are available on request — we do not invent scheme numbers on this page. Ask when you book if you need paperwork for insurance or landlord packs.

What should I do while I wait for an engineer?

Keep doors closed, move high-risk stock to a working cabinet if you have one, and note the displayed temperature and any alarm code. A photo of the data plate speeds parts checks before travel. Do not chip ice with a knife and do not keep resetting a high-pressure switch.

How fast can you attend in North London?

Emergency slots inside the 25 mile radius are typically two to four hours. Enfield, Barnet, Haringey, Camden and Islington are often quicker when an engineer is already on that ring. Coverage maps and borough pages live under coverage.

Do you repair cold rooms and freezer rooms?

Yes. Walk-in chillers, freezer rooms and remote plant are a core part of the diary, not an add-on. We probe temperatures, check doors and evaporators, and quote before a long leak search. Start with cold room repair London or freezer room repair London.

Do you repair ice machines?

Yes. Hoshizaki, Ice-O-Matic and other trade ice makers. Scale, blocked pumps and failed harvest cycles are common. We will not leave a machine producing unsafe ice after a hygiene fault. Book via ice machine repair London.

Will you recharge a leaking system without finding the leak?

No. We will not recharge a leaking system and drive away. A weigh-in after a leak is not a top-up. You get the leak-search plan and lead time before extra parts. That is an F-Gas and food-safety issue, not a shortcut.

Is VAT included in the price?

Business customers should expect VAT on call-out, labour and parts. Ask for a VAT invoice if accounts need it. Price bands quoted on the phone are usually ex VAT unless we say otherwise.

Can you work to a hotel or group purchase order?

Yes, if you say so when you book. If accounts need a PO before parts, tell us on 0203 952 4822 so the engineer is not waiting in a loading bay with no paper.

What if the cabinet cannot be repaired?

You still pay the diagnosis visit. You should get a clear stop-or-replace verdict before extra parts are ordered. We will not fit a compressor into a rusted liner just to take the ticket. Paying for diagnosis and then stopping is cheaper than a motor in a cabinet that will leak next month.

Do you offer planned maintenance as well as emergency call-out?

Yes. PPM and planned servicing on trade plant sits alongside 24/7 repair. Condenser cleans and probe checks cost less than dumped stock after an EHO visit. Ask on the call if you want a service calendar as well as a one-off repair.

Postcode, make and model from the data plate, whether it is a fridge, freezer, cold room or ice machine, displayed temperature or alarm, and access or parking notes. A photo of the plate helps. Then we can talk in a band before gauges go on.
