Ice machine service London

Ice machine service London is PPM: descale, filter, drain and a harvest test so cubes stay food-safe. Ice cream machine service London on the same site is a different barrel job, but hotels often book both on one visit. Servicing hard London water before a wedding weekend stops a bin of fused ice. A service contract puts cube and flake machines on a diary instead of a Friday panic. We still repair failed ice makers 24/7. Planned servicing is how bars pass an EHO ice check.

Common ice machine faults in London kitchens and cellars: no ice, small or cloudy cubes, a bin that is full of water, a scale-blocked evaporator, a failed water inlet valve, a noisy condenser, a drain that backs up into the bin, or a machine that runs but never harvests. Scale from hard London water is the usual enemy. Grease from a kitchen extract sitting on the condenser is the second. We strip, descale, check pumps and probes, and test a harvest cycle before we leave.

We use a temperature probe on the evaporator and on stored ice when the job needs it, and we talk about hygiene because ice is food. An EHO can fail a pub for a slimy ice machine as quickly as for a warm fridge. We will tell you if the bin needs a deep clean you should schedule, not just a parts swap.
