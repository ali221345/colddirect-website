Common faults we fix

  • Blocked honeycomb / return air: product pushed to the back of a multideck starves the coil and the case warms from the ends.
  • Iced evaporator on a dairy cabinet: a failed defrost or a torn curtain lets moist air in; ice then air, then dumped yoghurt.
  • Island freezer overfill: stock above the load line, lids that will not seat, compressor short-cycling.
  • Night blind left open: the case ran all night against store heat; we recover temperature and show the manager the blind.
  • Remote pack trip: one roof condensing unit feeding several supermarket cases; we isolate the failed case then the pack.
  • Leaking door furniture on glass merchandisers: split gaskets on drinks and dairy glass-doors pull in store air.
