Walk-in cold rooms are the backbone of a trade kitchen

We provide both repair and service / maintenance for walk-in cold rooms. A cold room is not a large fridge. It is a plant room with a door. When the evaporator ices, the door heater fails, or the condensing unit on the roof trips, every shelf in that room starts to warm. Restaurants, pubs with food, hotels, butchers, sandwich production and supermarket back rooms all depend on that space staying in the chill band. Cold room repair is therefore one of the first numbers a duty manager should have on the wall.

Cold room service London

Cold room service London is the scheduled visit that keeps a walk-in legal between emergencies. PPM servicing covers evaporator fans, door gaskets, strip curtains, drain lines and the remote condenser on the roof. We probe the room, log temperatures for HACCP, and flag a weak compressor before the Saturday rush. A service contract for a butchery or hotel walk-in is cheaper than a full thaw. We still run 24/7 cold room repair when the plant has already tripped. Servicing is how you avoid that call most months.

Common Walk-In Cold Room Faults & Controller Alarms We Fix Same-Day

  • High Temperature Alarm (HA / Hi) & Room Climbing Above +8°C: Caused by refrigerant leaks, tripped high-pressure switches on rooftop condensing units, seized condenser fan motors, or failed liquid-line solenoid valves.
  • Evaporator Coil Icing Up Solid: Faulty off-cycle or electric defrost termination probes (P2/Pb2), failed evaporator fan motors, blocked condensate drain lines, or split walk-in door gaskets drawing humid kitchen air.
  • Digital Controller Faults (Dixell XR20CX/XR60CX, Carel Easy, Eliwell ID Plus): Sensor probe errors (P1, P2, E0, E1), door switch alarms (dA), and burnt compressor relay contacts replaced from van stock.
  • HACCP & EHO Compliance Documentation: Every cold room repair in London includes calibrated air and core product temperature logging, F-Gas leak testing certification, and written engineer service sheets.

Common Commercial Breakdowns We Fix Same-Day

Our North & Central London response vans carry diagnostic gauges, OEM sensors, fan motors and F-Gas refrigerants to resolve critical kitchen breakdowns on the first visit:

1. Compressor Failures & Overload Trips

Diagnosing seized, short-cycling or overheating Embraco, Danfoss and Secop compressors, start relays, run capacitors and high-head-pressure trips.

2. Evaporator Fan & Airflow Faults

Replacing seized evaporator and condenser fan motors, clearing solid ice blocks across coils, and restoring uniform airflow across all cabinet shelves.

3. Thermostat & Digital Controller Errors

Fixing Carel, Dixell and Eliwell digital controllers displaying HA, Hi, P1, P2 or dEF probe alarms and erratic temperature hunting.

4. Split Door Gaskets & Frame Heaters

Stopping warm kitchen air ingress, heavy frost build-up and sweating door frames by fitting tight commercial magnetic door seals and mullion heaters.

5. F-Gas Refrigerant Leak Detection & Recharge

Electronic nitrogen pressure testing, brazing split copper lines, replacing filter driers and weighing in virgin R290, R404A, R448A, R449A or R134a charges.

6. Blocked Condensate Drains & Defrost Heaters

Clearing overflowing drip trays, replacing burnt-out electric defrost elements and termination thermostats so coils stay ice-free during peak service.
