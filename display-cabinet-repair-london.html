Common Display Cabinet Faults We Fix in London

  • Air curtain collapsed on an open deck. Blocked return honeycomb or a missing night blind lets kitchen air pour into the well. The thermometer at the back still looks fine while the front row is above eight degrees. We clear the grille, set the blind and prove the curtain with a probe, not a guess.
  • Glass door sweating and icing at the frame. A dead door heater or a torn magnetic gasket lets moist shop air in. Ice then builds on the inner pane and the door will not close. We test the heater tape and replace the gasket so the merchandiser can be opened without a scraper.
  • Serve-over well warm while the coil is still running. A failed evaporator fan under the deck, or a capillary restriction, leaves the well sluggish. Staff pile extra trays on the return, which makes it worse. We free the fan, check superheat, and only recover gas if a leak is proved.
