Why Your Fridge Stopped Working

  • Fridge running warm or not holding temperature. Usually a low gas charge from a slow leak, a failed thermostat, or a condenser so caked in kitchen grease it cannot reject heat. We check all three on the first visit rather than guessing at one part.
  • Compressor cycling on and off constantly. Short-cycling points to a fan fault, a blocked condenser, or a controller with the wrong set point. Left alone it burns out the compressor, turning a small repair into a full replacement.
  • Water pooling under the cabinet. A blocked or frozen drain line backs condensate up into the cabinet base. We clear the line and check the drain heater so it does not freeze again the same week.
