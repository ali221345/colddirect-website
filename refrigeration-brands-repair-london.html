Start with the plate, not the logo on the door

Door badges get swapped. Controllers get replaced. A “Williams” cabinet can be running a later aftermarket board. We photograph the rating plate for model, serial, refrigerant and running current before we talk parts. WhatsApp that plate to 07983 759320 before we travel if you want the van stocked. True

True merchandisers and kitchen cabinets are common in cafés and hotel lobbies. Glass-door heaters, self-closing hardware and a condenser stuffed behind a grille of till rolls are the London pattern. Why mixed-brand sites fail in a pattern, not at random

A basement with no make-up air cooks every integral condenser in the room, whatever the badge. A power cut resets every controller to a factory set point that is useless for high-risk food. A cleaner who jets the floor soaks every drain. Brand pages help you find the gasket profile. The plant-room story is often shared.
