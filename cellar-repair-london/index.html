What temperature should a pub beer cellar be kept at in London?

Standard UK pub cellars must hold a steady 11–13°C (52–55°F) for cask conditioned ales and keg storage, while remote glycol/ice-bank line chillers pull draught lager down to 2–6°C at the tap.
