Frequently Asked Questions

Does cellar repair mean structural basement repair?

No. This blueprint concerns beer-cellar cooling and environmental performance, not building or damp-proofing work.

Why is one part of my beer cellar warmer?

Airflow, insulation, door leakage, equipment heat and cooler sizing can create uneven cellar temperatures.

What is a cellar heat-load calculation?

It estimates heat entering the room from surfaces, air leakage, stored products and operating equipment.

Can a larger cooler solve every warm cellar?

No. Insulation, airflow, humidity and heat sources should be assessed before selecting cooling capacity.
