# ColdDirect Competitive Research (UK Commercial Refrigeration)

**Date:** 2026-09-16  
**Branch:** `ai/cold-direct-growth-upgrade`  
**ColdDirect model:** Commercial refrigeration **repair / emergency callout** (trade only, North London + 25 mile radius). **Not** an equipment e-commerce shop.

> Important: Many SERPs mix **repair** intent with **buy equipment** intent. Strategies from suppliers (Fridgesmart, Corr Chilled, Adexa shop) must **not** be copied as a product catalogue onto ColdDirect. Extract only UX, trust, IA and conversion patterns that fit a repair business.

## 3. Equipment suppliers (different market — patterns only)

Analysed for IA/UX patterns, **not** for cloning (15+ sites sampled across repair + supply):

| Site | Pattern useful for ColdDirect |
|------|-------------------------------|
| Fridgesmart | Deep category tree (multideck, bottle cooler, serve-over) |
| Corr Chilled | Subcategory clarity + lease messaging (ignore finance for CD) |
| Barcare / Adexa shop | Spec tables, related products |
| Foster Fridge (dealer) | Brand-first landing pages |
| EasyEquipment / Prodis / Koolmax | Filter + price UX — **out of scope** for repair site |

**Do not implement:** product grids, prices, stock, finance, lease-to-buy, SKU Product Offer schema — unless ColdDirect sells real inventory.

**May adapt:** breadcrumb consistency, FAQ near CTAs, “related services” blocks, brand landing pages (Liebherr gap closed in continue branch).

---

## 6. Conclusions (actionable)

1. ColdDirect should compete as **best-in-class London trade repair**, not as a fridge shop.
2. First wins are **indexability + sitemap hygiene + hub depth + trust facts**, not redesign.
3. Do not mass-create city doorway pages; deepen North London / 25-mile truth and a few high-intent equipment/brand pages.
4. Any guarantee, F-Gas number, or review aggregate must be human-verified before publishing.
5. Supplier UX (filters, Product schema with prices) is largely **out of scope**.
