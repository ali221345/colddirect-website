Cold room not freezing

A commercial cold room not freezing is a stock emergency for restaurants, pubs, takeaways, cafes, shops and hotels. Walk-in freezers should hold about minus 18. If product is soft, boxes are wet or the display sits at minus 5, isolate the load under your HACCP rules and call. Common causes are iced evaporators, failed evaporator fans, a door that is not sealing, a condenser packed with kitchen grease, or a refrigerant leak. Do not chip ice off the coil. Do not add gas yourself. Cold Direct diagnoses walk-in plant on site, then prices before parts. We do not do household repairs. For icing on cabinets as well as rooms, read why a commercial freezer ices up.

Ice build up, door seal and fan motor

Ice build up on a walk-in evaporator blocks airflow so the room looks warm even while the compressor runs. Ice on the door frame usually means a torn gasket, a failed door heater or a door left on the latch during service. A fan motor that has seized or thrown a blade leaves dead spots in the room: one rack frozen, another dripping. We test fans, heaters, drains and gaskets before we talk about compressors. If ice returns the day after a defrost, stop scraping and book a commercial engineer.

Gas leak and panel damage

A gas leak on a cold room often shows as a stripe of ice on the coil, oil around a joint, or a room that never quite pulls down after a recent “top-up”. F-Gas work must follow leak-find-then-charge, not weekly regas. Read F-Gas regulations for commercial refrigeration before anyone opens a cylinder. Panel damage — crushed corners, open cam-locks, wet insulation — lets kitchen heat in and makes the plant run flat out. We repair doors, hinges, heaters and panels on modular rooms as well as the refrigeration circuit. Call 07983 759320 for 24/7 commercial attendance in London, typically 60 to 90 minutes.

FAQ

Why is my walk-in cold room not freezing?

Ice on the evaporator, a failed fan motor, a door that is not sealing, a blocked condenser or a refrigerant leak. We diagnose the room, not guess from one alarm. Commercial walk-ins only.

Do you repair damaged cold room panels and doors?

Yes. Panel dents, failed cam-locks, torn door seals and door heaters are commercial cold room repair. We do not repair domestic house freezers.
