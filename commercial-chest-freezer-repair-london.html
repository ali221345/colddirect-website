Why Your Chest Freezer Stopped Working?

  • Baskets stacked proud of the load line block return air to the evaporator, so the lid ices while the core of boxed product stays soft.
  • A thermostat capillary crushed behind the liner, often after a metal basket was forced down, leaves the compressor off even though the well only feels cool to the hand.
  • Oil logged in the suction after the chest was tipped during a shop fit-out makes the compressor run hot and trip on thermal within minutes of a reset.
