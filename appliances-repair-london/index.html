Appliances Repair London - Your Complete Commercial Solution

If you search appliances repair London for commercial refrigeration, this is the pillar page. Cold Direct is not a domestic white-goods network. We repair trade appliances: the cabinets and plant rooms Environmental Health cares about. From a small under-counter fridge to a walk-in freezer room, we attend 24/7 from North London across London. Start here, then open the service page that names your machine. Sister pages: commercial appliances repair London, freezer room repair London and catering repair London.

Managers type “appliances repair” when they do not yet know whether the job is a fridge, a freezer room or a cellar pack. We still need the make, the current temperature, and whether fans are turning. We quote on site. We do not take house fridge-freezers, washing machines or hobs. If that is the job, we will say so on the phone and save you a wasted call-out. A Saturday thaw in a restaurant walk-in is not the same job as a quiet off-licence bottle well, but both are commercial refrigeration appliances and both sit on this pillar page until you pick the named service.

On a typical call we ask three things: what the display reads, whether the fans are turning, and whether the door still seals. That tells us if we are looking at airflow, a control fault, or a compressor that has already given up. We are F-Gas certified. We will not recharge a leaking system and leave. We find the leak, repair it if the plant is worth saving, then charge to the manufacturer’s weight. That is appliances repair done as refrigeration work, not as a top-up in the car park.
