Warm bottles lose you the round

We provide both repair and service / maintenance for back-bar bottle coolers. A back-bar bottle cooler is a small cabinet with a hard life. Doors slam all night. Condensers sit against a hot wall. Glass gets stacked against the fans. When it fails, pubs, bars, clubs and takeaways sell warm lager and lose the next round. Restaurants and hotel bars have the same unit under the back bar. Bottle cooler repair is commercial work. Cold Direct does not repair kitchen fridges in houses. We are North London Commercial Refrigeration Engineers covering London and a 25 mile radius into Hertfordshire and Essex.

Bottle cooler service London

Bottle cooler service London is a short PPM visit: pull the unit if we can, clean the condenser, check sliding doors and probe a bottle, not the display. Servicing after a deep clean of the back bar stops lime and dust choking the fans before Friday. A service contract for a group of pubs puts every cooler on a round so one site is not always the emergency. We still repair failed cabinets 24/7. Planned servicing just means more cold bottles when the bar is full.

Typical faults: the sliding door is off the track and the seal is gone; the condenser is blocked with dust and lime; one side of a double door unit is warm; the thermostat is iced; lights work but the compressor does not; water pools under the bar from a blocked drip. We pull the unit out if we can, clean the condenser, check fans and charge, and test bottle temperature, not just the air display.
