Common True Fridge Faults We Fix in London

  • Reversing condenser fan seized. True uses reverse rotation to shed dust. When the motor sticks, the coil loads and the fridge trips. We replace the fan motor and clean the coil so the reverse cycle can work again.
  • Self-close cam worn on a heavy glass door. The leaf rests open a centimetre. Shop heat floods in and the evaporator ices. We fit the cam kit and check the gasket magnet.
  • Capillary restriction on a T-series. One section of the cabinet is warm while the other still holds. We recover, replace the drier and cap tube if needed, and weigh in the charge from the plate, not by eye.
