What should a commercial fridge repair quote include?

Compare the total scope rather than the headline call-out price. One quote may include diagnostic time while another lists attendance and labour separately. Ask whether a return visit is included if a part must be ordered.

Questions to ask before approving a repair

Attendance and diagnosisThe initial charge, time included and charges if you decline the repair.

Why two fridges can need different repair budgets

The make and model identify which components fit the cabinet. A prep counter, upright cabinet and display fridge can require different parts even when each has the same symptom. Send a clear photograph of the model plate, including the serial number, when asking about a visit.

Access also matters. Tell the engineer if equipment is built into a counter, located in a basement or only accessible outside service hours. Describe any loading restrictions and who can provide access. These details help define the work before the appointment.

A warm cabinet is a symptom, not a diagnosis. Avoid assuming it needs a new compressor or a refrigerant refill from the display temperature alone. Ask the engineer to explain the diagnosed fault, proposed work and alternatives before approving the repair.

Repair or replace: compare the full cost

Start with the condition of the cabinet and its repair history. A single repair on otherwise suitable equipment is a different decision from repeated breakdowns on a damaged cabinet. Ask about parts availability and the expected completion time, not just the component price.

For a replacement, include delivery, installation, removal of the old unit and any changes needed for the new equipment. Check that the replacement meets your capacity, dimensions and operating requirements. Compare warranty terms in writing; do not assume every quote includes the same cover.

There is no reliable percentage rule that suits every business. Weigh the complete quotes alongside disruption to service and how long you expect to keep using the equipment. For freezers, use our commercial freezer repair information so you are comparing the right equipment.

Prepare these details before calling

  • Your business postcode, opening hours and site contact.
  • The fridge make, model and serial number, or a photograph of the data plate.
  • The displayed temperature, any error code and when the problem started.
  • Whether the fault is continuous or happens at particular times.
  • Access, parking and purchase-order requirements.
  • Who can authorise the diagnosis and any subsequent repair.

For initial troubleshooting information, read commercial fridge not cooling. Keep checks to those permitted in the manufacturer's user instructions. Leave electrical and refrigerant work to a suitably qualified engineer.

Frequently asked questions

How much does commercial fridge repair cost in London?

The total depends on the diagnosis, parts, labour, access and appointment time. Ask for the attendance charge and what it includes before booking, then an itemised repair quote after diagnosis.

Can I get an exact price over the phone?

A model number and fault description help with initial advice, but the cause may need an on-site diagnosis. Confirm whether any figure provided before inspection is an estimate or a fixed quote.

Should I repair or replace my commercial fridge?

Compare the complete repair quote with the installed cost of a suitable replacement. Consider cabinet condition, previous faults, parts availability, downtime and the warranty terms offered.

What if I decide not to proceed with the repair?

Ask before booking which attendance and diagnostic charges remain payable if you decline the repair or the cabinet cannot be repaired. Confirm approval arrangements before additional work or parts are ordered.
