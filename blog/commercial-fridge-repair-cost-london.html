Call-out and diagnosis, not “free look”

The first line on a 2026 trade invoice is attendance. In Greater London that usually sits in a £90 to £150 band ex VAT for a daytime visit that includes a temperature survey, condenser access, and a yes/no on whether the fault is airflow, electrics, a leak or a compressor. You can decline the repair after that verdict. You cannot decline the visit if the van has already sat in congestion and the engineer has already opened the grille. Marketing that shouts “free call-out” often recovers the cost in inflated parts. Ask what the first hour includes before the van is booked.

Night, Sunday and bank-holiday emergency work is not the daytime band. Expect roughly 1.25× to 1.5× labour when a restaurant needs a two-to-four-hour slot after last orders. Parking, loading-bay waits and a missing plant-room key add time. Time is labour. Tell us about access on the call so the quote on site is not a surprise.

Labour after you say yes

Once you authorise the repair, extra labour typically sits around £90 to £140 per hour ex VAT depending on hour of day. A gasket and a condenser clean often finish inside the first hour. A leak search on a remote line in a ceiling void does not. A compressor swap is a longer job and a different parts ticket. We stop and re-quote if the job grows beyond what we described. That is how a commercial fridge repair cost stays a decision, not a blank cheque.

What actually moves the invoice

Access: a fridge built into a bar run costs more labour than a free-standing upright you can pull out. Built-in means side panels, a kick-plate and sometimes a floor that has swollen around the feet. Brand: Foster, Williams, True and Gram parts are not interchangeable even when the fault looks the same. Refrigerant: a weigh-in after a leak is not a “top-up”. Time of day: 02:00 in Soho is not 10:00 in Enfield. Stock at risk does not change the price of a fan motor, but it should change how fast you call. Multi-cabinet plant racks in hotels take longer to diagnose because we have to map which solenoid belongs to which room.

A purchase order from a hotel group is not optional wording. If accounts need a PO before parts, say so when you book 0203 952 4822 so the engineer is not waiting in a loading bay with a compressor on the van and no paper.

Repair versus replace

Use repair when the chassis, liner and doors are sound and the fault is a fan, gasket, drain, controller or a first compressor on a well-kept cabinet. Use replace when the liner is rusted through, the doors will not seal even with a new gasket, or the quote for a compressor plus gas plus labour is close to a new trade upright with a warranty. We will write that comparison on site. Paying for diagnosis and then stopping is cheaper than fitting a new motor into a cabinet that will leak next month.

How to keep the bill smaller without skipping safety

Clean the condenser weekly in takeaways and pubs. Keep cardboard off the rear grille. Replace a torn gasket before ice hides a leak. Have the model and serial from the data plate when you call. Have a manager who can approve parts. Have parking or loading information. Those habits save more than haggling a tenner off labour. Do not chip ice with a knife. Do not keep resetting a high-pressure switch. Those two habits turn a first-visit commercial fridge repair cost into a compressor replacement.
