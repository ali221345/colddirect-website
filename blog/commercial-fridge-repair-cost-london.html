Why commercial prices are not domestic prices

Trade cabinets use different controls, thicker insulation, Gastronorm pans and F-Gas circuits. Night call-outs for restaurants, pubs, takeaways, cafes, shops and hotels cost more than a weekday shop visit because the diary and parts van are different. Parking, congestion and access in Zone 1 add time. A “cheap” online quote that ignores the model plate is not a commercial quote. Ask what is included: diagnosis, first hour, parts, gas, VAT and warranty on the agreed work.

What changes the bill

Access: a fridge built into a bar run costs more labour than a free-standing upright. Brand: Gram and Foster parts can lead-time. Refrigerant: older R404A work is tighter under F-Gas rules than newer blends. Time: 2 a.m. in a Soho restaurant is not 10 a.m. in Enfield. Stock risk does not change the parts price, but it should change how fast you call. Shops with open display decks and hotels with multiple cabinets on one plant rack need a longer diagnosis.

Call-out versus “free diagnosis”

A genuine diagnosis on a commercial fridge means gauges or equivalent tests, condenser inspection, and a decision on gas versus electrics. Marketing that says “free call-out” often recovers the cost in inflated parts. Cold Direct states call-out plus diagnosis in the £90–£150 band, then labour if you approve the repair. You can decline the repair after diagnosis. You cannot decline physics: a seized compressor will not get cheaper overnight.

How to keep the invoice smaller

Clean condensers weekly in takeaways and pubs. Replace torn gaskets before ice hides a leak. Tell the engineer the model, serial, symptoms and whether the fault followed a power cut. Have a manager on site who can approve parts. Have parking or loading information ready. Those five habits save more money than haggling a tenner off labour.

VAT, warranty and purchase orders

Business customers should expect VAT on the invoice. Ask how warranty applies to the agreed commercial repair, not to food already lost. Hotels and groups often need a PO before work. Say so on the call so the engineer is not waiting in a loading bay. Payment terms are confirmed when you book.
