A fridge room is a walk-in chiller. Staff walk in, pick fresh produce, and the air should stay in the chill band. Ice on the walls usually means a defrost or door fault, not that the room is meant to be a freezer. Cold Direct repairs commercial fridge rooms across Greater London. F-Gas certified. 24/7. +3°C fridge room vs -18°C freezer room

Fridge rooms store fresh food. Freezer rooms store frozen stock. The plant, the set point and the photo on the page should match. If your room is frozen storage, use freezer room repair.

Common fridge room faults

Door left on the latch, torn gaskets, iced coils after a stock-up, failed evaporator fans, a dirty roof condenser, or a controller that lost its set point after a power cut.
