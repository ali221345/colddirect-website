Common Faults We Fix

  • E5 / E7 freeze fault - barrel overload, mix too thick, or a beater that has seized. We clear the fault after we test, we do not just reset the board.
  • Not freezing - warm hopper, dirty condenser, low gas or a weak compressor. Product probe first, then plant.
  • Leaking above or below the plunger - door gaskets, O-rings and spigot seals that were not replaced at the last clean.
  • Mix leaking into the drip drawer - worn beater shaft seal. Mix in the tray is a hygiene fail as well as a parts job.
  • Compressor starts then stops - high head pressure from a blocked condenser, a failed fan, or an overload that trips in a hot shopfront.
  • No dispense - frozen barrel, air lock, or a plunger that will not open. We thaw and test before we quote a pump.
  • Soft ice - mix too warm, worn scraper blades, or a refrigeration circuit that cannot pull the barrel down.
  • Noisy condenser - fan motor, bent blades, or sugar dust packed in the coil. We clean and rebalance airflow on the first visit where we can.
