Common Commercial Fridge Faults

The faults we log on commercial fridge repair london jobs are a short list. A dirty condenser in a hot kitchen is still the Saturday classic: grease and flour cake the coil, head pressure rises, and the cabinet cannot hold 1 to 4 °C. An iced evaporator after a door left on the latch starves airflow. Torn gaskets let moist air in until the coil ices and the display climbs. Evaporator fans seize. Controllers lose their set point after a power cut. Compressors weaken and then trip. Low refrigerant after a leak looks like “it just got warm” until we weigh the charge.

If a trade cabinet is running but product sits at 10 °C, do not keep adding stock. We measure air on and off the coil before we talk parts.
