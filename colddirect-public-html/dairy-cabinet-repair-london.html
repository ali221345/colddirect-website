Common Dairy Cabinet Faults We Fix in London

  • Milk crates jammed against the return grille. Staff park stock on the intake. The curtain folds and the top shelf climbs while the base still feels cold. We clear the path, reset the load line, and probe each shelf before we leave.
  • Condensate tray overflowing onto dairy. A blocked drain or a failed tray heater dumps water onto cartons. That is an EHO issue as well as a refrigeration fault. We rod the drain, check heat tape, and sanitise the tray so the case can trade.
  • Expansion valve hunting. A sticky valve or a starved distributor makes temperature swing from two to ten degrees in a cycle. Product at the ends of the deck is the first to go. We set superheat and only recover gas if a leak is found on the joints.
