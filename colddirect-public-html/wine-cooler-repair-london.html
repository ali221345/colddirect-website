Service wine has to be at temperature, not “nearly”

A commercial wine cooler is a display and a storage unit. Restaurants, hotels, wine bars and off-licences hold white, sparkling and sometimes red at set bands. When the cabinet warms, you lose by-the-glass service and you risk stock. This is not a domestic kitchen wine rack. Cold Direct repairs commercial wine coolers only. We are North London Commercial Refrigeration Engineers covering London and a 25 mile radius across Hertfordshire and Essex.

Dual-zone cabinets fail on one zone while the other still looks fine. Glass-door units ice on the evaporator and then flood. Controllers lose their set point after a power cut. Condensers behind a tight bar void block with dust. Door seals on constantly opened pass cabinets perish. We test both zones, check fans, and look at how the unit is ventilated. A wine cooler pushed tight to a plasterboard wall will keep failing in summer.

Fine dining in Westminster, the City, Camden and Islington cannot serve warm sparkling. Hotels need the lobby and restaurant cabinets working for events. Off-licences in Stratford, Romford, Ilford and Walthamstow need glass-door merchandisers that hold overnight.
