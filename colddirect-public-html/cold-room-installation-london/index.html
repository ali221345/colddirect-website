How we design and size a commercial cold room

A cold room that is too small warms up every time the door opens; one that is too large burns capital and electricity. Our installation process starts with a free site survey and a measured heat-load calculation — not a guess from a brochure. We count the door openings per hour, the product throughput, the kitchen's ambient temperature and any glass-fronted display load, then size the insulation and the refrigeration to match.

Design stepWhat we decide and why it matters
Heat-load surveyProduct volume, door traffic and ambient peaks sized into a kW duty figure
Panel specification80–100 mm PIR panels for chilled rooms, 100–150 mm for freezers, cam-locked and sealed
Refrigeration plantSplit or monoblock system sized with defrost strategy for the duty — never oversized "to be safe"
Floor & drainageInsulated floor specs, falls and drain heaters where wash-down is needed
Controls & alarmsCarel or Dixell controllers with high/low temperature alarms wired to alert you
CommissioningPull-down test to temperature, F-Gas record, operating manual and handover certificate

Cold room installation FAQs

How much does a commercial cold room installation cost in London?

Every installation is quoted as a fixed package — survey, panels, plant, electrical work, commissioning and certification in one written price. The design consultation is a fixed-rate visit credited against the installation.

How long does a cold room installation take?

A typical walk-in room installs in 2–4 working days once panels and plant are on site; a like-for-like replacement can be phased over two nights to protect your trading schedule.

Do you handle F-Gas and legal paperwork?

Yes. Refrigerant handling is done by F-Gas certified engineers with full recovery records, and you receive commissioning certificates and operating documentation for your EHO and HACCP files.

Can you size the room properly for a dark kitchen or restaurant?

That is the core of the service: we run a measured heat-load calculation from door traffic, product throughput and ambient temperature, then size panels and plant to the duty — typically saving 20–30% on running electricity versus an oversized kit room.

Do you repair cold rooms as well as install them?

Yes — 24/7 emergency repair and planned servicing across London for the rooms we install and the ones we inherit.

Is the design consultation really fixed-price?

Yes: one agreed fee for the site survey and written design, credited in full against the installation when you proceed.
