Technical diagnostics our Soho engineers run first

Every Soho call-out starts behind the bar, on the roof or inside the controller — not with a guess. Our F-Gas certified engineers work on basement walk-in chillers, serve-over counters and bottle coolers across W1, and we fault-find in a fixed order so you get a priced repair decision the same visit. These are the controller fault codes we see most around Soho's late-night venues:

Controller / code What it means on site First action we take
Carel E2 / E3 High or low temperature alarm — cabinet drifting out of range Check probe position, fan operation and condenser airflow before touching gas
Carel E4 / E5 Defrost or probe fault — ice build-up on the evaporator Force a defrost cycle, test heaters, verify the termination probe
Dixell XR06 / XR03 Parameter or sensor error after a power cut Re-read sensors against a calibrated thermometer, restore set points
Eliwell IDPlus AL1/AL2 Generic alarm logged overnight Pull the alarm history — it usually records the hour the plant started failing

Low refrigerant after a pinhole leak is the single most common root cause we find in serving Wardour Street, Dean Street, Brewer Street and the Chinatown walk-in chillers off Gerrard Street. We pressure-test with trace gas, repair the leak, vacuum the circuit and recharge by weight — a legal F-Gas process, never a disposable top-up. Compressor electricals get a full check on every Soho job: start capacitor, run current against the data plate, and winding resistance. If a compressor is tight or the valves are passing, we quote a replacement with the recovery and reclamation paperwork your EHO inspection and Westminster licensing expectations demand.
