Common Williams Fridge Faults We Fix in London

  • LAE or Dixell probe drifted off true. The screen shows plus two while a food probe in the cabinet reads eight. Chefs keep turning the set point down and ice the evaporator. We replace the sensor, calibrate, and leave a written air and product reading.
  • Pipework stressed at the drier after a cabinet was dragged on seized castors. A small oil stain at the joint is a leak waiting to empty the charge. We repair the joint, pressure test, and only then weigh in gas. Pushing a Williams on locked wheels is how that crack starts.
  • Evaporator fan delay stuck after the door closes. The timer never restarts the fans, so the cabinet looks quiet and product at the top warms. We test the door switch and the delay board rather than condemning the compressor.
