Walk-in chiller, not freezer

The door sign should read chiller or cold room. The controller set point is usually +1°C to +5°C. Fresh vegetables stay crisp. A freezer room is a different job: ice, frozen boxes and a much lower set point. Tell us which room failed when you call.

Common walk-in chiller faults

Dirty remote condensers, blocked evaporator airflow, torn door gaskets, failed fans and low refrigerant after a leak. We probe the room, check the door seal and quote before a long repair.
