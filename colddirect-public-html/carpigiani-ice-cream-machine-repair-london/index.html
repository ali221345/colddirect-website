Carpigiani fault-finding: alarms, beater systems and refrigeration

We repair Batch freezers such as the Carpigiani LB-100/LB-200 series, and showcases across London for gelaterias, cafés, dessert parlours and event caterers. A Carpigiani machine rarely dies quietly — it usually warns you first: the beater motor overload, low viscosity or high pressure alarms on the display. Our engineers read those signals in order:

Symptom / alarmLikely causeHow we fix it
Beater motor overloadFrozen mix around the beater, worn scraper blades dragging, or a tired capacitorStrip and inspect the barrel, replace scrapers, test motor current against the plate
Low viscosity / slow freezeRefrigerant undercharge after a leak, or a partial TXV blockagePressure-test the circuit, locate the leak, recharge by weight under F-Gas rules
High pressure cut-outCondenser choked with dust or a failed fanDeep-clean the condenser, verify fan direction and airflow, re-set the high-pressure switch
Hopper temperature warningHopper thermostat or feed-tube hygiene issueTest the thermostat against a calibrated probe and advise on the mix handling routine

Because these are catering machines in front of customers, we also carry the wear parts that end Carpigiani downtime early: scraper blades, door gaskets, mix feed tubes, o-rings, drive belts and common control boards. On a first visit we aim to leave the machine churning, not order a part and vanish.
