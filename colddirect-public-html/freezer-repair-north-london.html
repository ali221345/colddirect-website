Protect stock before attendance

Record the temperature, keep the door closed and use verified alternative frozen storage if required. Do not chip ice from the evaporator or repeatedly reset a tripping unit. Tell the engineer about alarms and recent power interruptions.

Cabinet and freezer-room repairs

We check door sealing, evaporator airflow, defrost operation, controls and refrigeration performance before quoting. The service covers upright and chest cabinets as well as walk-in freezer rooms used by restaurants, shops and caterers.
