Common Foster Fridge Faults We Fix in London

  • G3 or Eco controller in door-open lockout. A failed door switch keeps the fans off and the alarm on even when the leaf is shut. Chefs tape the switch, which ices the coil. We fit the correct switch and clear the alarm properly.
  • Integral condenser filter mat removed and never refitted. Without the mat, grease goes straight onto the coil. Head pressure climbs and the Foster trips on HP. We clean the coil, fit a mat, and show the kitchen where it lives.
  • Evaporator fans dead after a switch fault. The compressor may still run, so the case feels “on” while the top shelf warms. We test both fans and the delay, not just the compressor amps.
