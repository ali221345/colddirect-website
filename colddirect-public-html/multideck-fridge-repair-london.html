Common multideck faults

Blocked return air, dirty honeycomb, failed evaporator fans, night blinds left up and collapsed air curtains are the shop list. Condensers packed with dust behind a gondola raise head pressure. Controllers lose set point. Low refrigerant after a leak warms the top shelf first. We measure air on and off the coil before we talk parts.
