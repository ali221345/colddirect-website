Do you offer 24/7 commercial refrigeration repair in London?

Yes. Typical emergency attendance is two to four hours across our 25 mile North London radius. A warm walk-in on a Saturday night is treated as an emergency, not a Monday ticket.

Is Cold Direct commercial only?

Yes. We repair trade kitchens, shops and hotels. Household fridge-freezers are not the default diary. If you are unsure whether your cabinet is commercial, send a photo of the data plate.

Do you quote before starting a repair?

Yes. We diagnose on site and quote before a long repair. If a part has to be ordered, you get the lead time before you commit. We will not leave a cabinet electrically unsafe or recharge a leaking system and drive away.

Which brands do you repair?

Foster, Williams, True, Gram, Polar, Adexa, Empire, Liebherr, Hoshizaki, Sub-Zero and most other catering brands. See the full list on brands.

Are engineers F-Gas certified?

Refrigerant handling on F-Gas systems is done by F-Gas competent engineers. Registration numbers are available on request — we do not invent scheme numbers on this page. Ask when you book if you need paperwork for insurance or landlord packs.

What should I do while I wait?

Keep doors closed, move high-risk stock to a working cabinet if you have one, and note the displayed temperature and any alarm code. A photo of the data plate speeds parts checks before travel.
