ColdDirect plans commercial cold room installation for London kitchens, shops and catering sites. We consider available space, access, ventilation, temperature requirements, stock flow and cleaning before specifying the room and refrigeration equipment.

Installation planning and commissioning

The work starts with site dimensions, door position, floor and drainage requirements, electrical supplies and condenser heat rejection. Equipment is selected for the intended duty rather than a one-size-fits-all package. On completion, controls, alarms, door operation and temperature performance are checked and explained to your team.

Chiller rooms and freezer rooms

Choose a walk-in chiller for fresh stock or a freezer room for frozen storage. We can also assess replacement of ageing panels, doors and refrigeration plant. Existing faults belong on our repair page; new rooms and upgrades are planned through this installation service.
