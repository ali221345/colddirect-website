Common commercial freezer faults we check

Several faults can cause a freezer to lose temperature. The engineer checks the unit before recommending a repair or replacement part.

  • Freezer not freezing / not cold enough. If the fan still runs but product sits at minus five instead of minus eighteen, we check the thermostat, the controller set point and the gas charge. Low refrigerant after a leak will not hold temperature.
  • Ice build-up and water leaking inside. A failed defrost heater leaves a solid block on the evaporator. Melt then pools on the floor. Torn door seals let moist kitchen air in, so ice returns the next night. We replace heaters, timers and gaskets rather than chipping ice and leaving.
  • Compressor noisy or not running. A rattling or silent compressor often means the motor or start gear has failed. We isolate that from a seized fan so you do not pay for the wrong part.

Commercial Freezer Controller Alarm Codes & Emergency Diagnostics

  • High Temperature Alarm (HA, Hi, AH): Triggered when cabinet air rises above threshold (typically above -12°C on a -18°C to -22°C freezer). We test compressor start gear, condenser fan airflow, refrigerant subcooling/superheat, and defrost termination relays.
  • Probe & Defrost Faults (P1, P2, E1, E2, dEF): Faulty evaporator termination probes or open-circuit electric defrost heaters on Foster, Williams, Polar, True and Gram cabinets replaced from van stock.
  • Door Heater & Gasket Failures: Frozen-shut doors or ice around the perimeter frame caused by failed mullion/perimeter heater tapes or split magnetic gaskets.
