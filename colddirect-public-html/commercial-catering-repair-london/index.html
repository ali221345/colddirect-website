Common Catering Breakdowns We Fix

Fridge not cooling is usually a dirty condenser, a failed evaporator fan, or a controller that lost its set point after a power cut. Ice build up on a catering freezer or prep well comes from torn gaskets, blocked drains, or staff chipping ice with a knife - we will not ask anyone to do that. Door seal failure lets warm kitchen air in and the plant runs flat out until the compressor trips. Compressor failure needs a clear diagnosis: we measure air on and off the coil before we talk about a replacement. A gas leak recharge is only done after we find and repair the leak; F-Gas work is certified, not a top-up in the car park.

We also see cellar plant rooms with blocked condensers, bottle coolers iced solid after a busy weekend, and walk-ins stacked so tight that airflow dies. On arrival we probe food if you need a HACCP note, inspect door furniture with a paper test, and quote in plain English. Then we fix.
