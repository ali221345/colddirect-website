ColdDirect diagnoses commercial Taylor ice cream machines across London. Tell us the model, error message, hopper temperature and whether the beaters and compressor run. Brand names are used for equipment identification; ColdDirect is an independent repair business.

Common Taylor machine symptoms

We assess soft product, inconsistent freezing, unusual noise, beater or drive problems, control messages and hopper temperature faults. Follow the manufacturer cleaning and safety instructions, and do not bypass interlocks or repeatedly clear an alarm without finding the cause.

Prepare for the engineer

Send a photograph of the data plate and control display when booking. Note when the machine was last cleaned and whether the fault follows a wash cycle, power interruption or product refill. This helps distinguish setup and maintenance issues from refrigeration or electrical faults.
