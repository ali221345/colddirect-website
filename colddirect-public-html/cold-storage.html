Walk-in chill rooms and catering cold rooms

Most restaurant and hotel stores in North London are walk-in chillers: boxed rooms with a ceiling evaporator, a remote or housed condensing unit, and a door that takes a beating. Common faults are iced coils after a wet load, a failed door heater that lets the gasket freeze, a blocked condenser on a backyard unit, or a controller that lost its set point after a power cut. We recover leak sites, weigh in refrigerant to the plate, and write air temperatures for your HACCP sheet.

Freezer rooms and mixed stores

A freezer room that will not pull down after a delivery is usually ice on the coil, a stuck defrost, or a door left on the latch. Product in the doorway does not count as a gas leak. We say so if that is the whole visit. If the room is a dedicated freeze store, use freezer room repair London or walk-in freezer room repair London. Mixed sites with both a chill room and a freeze room often share a pack; we isolate which circuit is in trouble before we open the system.
