ColdDirect attends commercial Carpigiani ice cream equipment in London. We check the reported symptom, operating conditions, controls and refrigeration performance before recommending repair. ColdDirect is independent; manufacturer names identify the equipment we are asked to assess.

Freezing and production faults

Soft product, slow freezing, ice build-up, inconsistent dispensing and unexpected stops can have different causes. Product mix, cleaning, ventilation and operating settings need to be considered alongside mechanical and refrigeration faults.

Book with useful machine details

Provide the exact model, any error code, the product temperature and what happens during a production cycle. Keep the machine off if it is electrically unsafe or repeatedly tripping. We will explain the diagnosis and repair options before work proceeds.
