Common faults we fix

  • Blocked outdoor coil: grease and lint on a shop condenser, high pressure, indoor units blowing warm.
  • Failed indoor fan or cassette pump: water on the ceiling, then a lockout. We clear the pump and the drain.
  • Refrigerant leak on a split or VRF branch: find, repair, pressure test, vacuum, charge. F-Gas logged.
  • PCB or sensor faults: a cassette that ice-balls then floods, or a controller that will not call the outdoor unit.
  • Isolator and compressor trips: tight bearings, low oil after a leak, or a condenser fan that has seized in heat.
  • Wrong indoor selection after a shop fit-out: we tell you if the unit is undersized rather than pretending gas will fix it.
