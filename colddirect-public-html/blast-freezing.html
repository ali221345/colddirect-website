FAQs

Do you repair house chest freezers?

No. This is trade blast and ice-cream plant only.

Can you attend if the cabinet is still under a manufacturer warranty?

We can inspect and tell you if the fault is use, installation or a sealed-system failure. Warranty paperwork is the manufacturer’s, not ours.

Do blast chillers and blast freezers share parts?

Fans, heaters and controllers overlap. Charge and programme do not. We treat them as separate machines on the same visit if both are down.
