If the display is warm, you cannot sell the food

Display cabinets are the shop window of chilled food. Serve-over counters in a deli or butchers, multideck cases in a supermarket, and glass-door uprights in a convenience store all have one job: hold product in the legal band while customers can still see it. When the air curtain collapses, the night blind is left up, or the evaporator ices, the top shelf cooks and the bottom shelf ices. That is a waste event and an EHO event. Display cabinet repair is therefore a same-day job for most retailers.

Cold Direct repairs commercial display refrigeration only. We are North London Commercial Refrigeration Engineers covering a 25 mile radius across London, Hertfordshire and Essex. We attend restaurants with dessert displays, pubs with food counters, sandwich shops, bakeries, takeaways with grab-and-go, and full supermarket aisles. Brands include Williams, Foster, True, Polar, Blizzard, Gram, Lec, Hoshizaki, Snomaster and Tefcold.

Faults we see on display cases: night blinds not used, overloaded shelves blocking return air, failed evaporator fans, dirty honeycomb on a serve-over, leaking door heaters on glass doors, and controllers that have been turned down so far the case ices then floods. We train the duty manager on loading lines while we are there. A repaired case that is immediately stacked above the load line will fail again by Friday.
