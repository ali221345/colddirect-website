About Cold Direct

Trade refrigeration repair across North London and a 25 mile radius. Commercial only. 24/7 emergency.

Who we are

Cold Direct is a commercial-only refrigeration repair company covering North London and a 25 mile radius across London, Hertfordshire and Essex. We attend restaurants, pubs, bars, takeaways, hotels, shops and staff kitchens. We do not run a domestic house-call diary as our main service.

When a cold room, freezer, display cabinet or ice machine fails, stock and an EHO visit are at risk. We quote before we start, we check temperatures with a probe, and we offer 24/7 emergency call-out. Brands we see every week include Williams, Foster, Polar, Hoshizaki, True, Blizzard and Adexa Direct.
