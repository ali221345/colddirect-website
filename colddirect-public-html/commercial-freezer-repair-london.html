Common Commercial Freezer Problems

The faults we log on commercial freezer repair london jobs are a short list. A dirty condenser in a hot kitchen is still the Saturday classic: grease and flour cake the coil, head pressure rises, and the cabinet cannot hold minus 18. An iced evaporator after a door left on the latch starves airflow. Torn gaskets and failed door heaters let moist air in until the coil is a block of ice. Evaporator fans seize. Controllers lose their set point after a power cut. Compressors weaken and then trip. Low refrigerant after a leak looks like “it just got warm” until we weigh the charge.

If you want freezer repair london for a trade cabinet that is not cooling, do not chip ice with a knife.
