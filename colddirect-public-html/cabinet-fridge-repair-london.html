Common Cabinet Fridge Faults We Fix in London

  • Grease-packed condenser behind the kick plate. Kitchen dust and oil mat the coil so head pressure climbs and the cabinet trips. Staff think the gas has gone. We brush or wash the condenser, check the fan, and only then look at the charge.
  • Warped gasket that lets the door suck in and ice the back wall. A torn or heat-set seal dumps moist air onto the evaporator. Ice grows on the liner and the thermostat never reaches cut-out. We fit the correct gasket profile, not a generic strip that peels in a week.
  • Controller blank after a power dip. Digital boards on trade uprights lose set point or lock in defrost. The fan may still run while the compressor stays off. We read the alarm, replace a failed probe, and prove plus three on a food simulant, not on the door thermometer.
