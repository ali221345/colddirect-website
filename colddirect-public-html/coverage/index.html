How we decide if we can attend

Give the site name, postcode, make if you can see the data plate, the last safe temperature, and whether the fans are turning. Typical emergency attendance inside inner North and central London is 60 to 90 minutes. Sites south of the river or on the westbound A40 take longer. Night and Sunday work is normal for hospitality. We quote on site after we test the plant. Household fridge-freezers are not the default diary.
