Technical diagnostics our North London engineers run first

Every North London call-out starts on the roof, behind the unit or inside the controller — not with a guess. Our F-Gas certified engineers work on split systems, cassette units and cellar air conditioning, and we fault-find in a fixed order so you get a priced repair decision the same visit. These are the controller fault codes we see most around N1–N22:

Controller / codeWhat it means on siteFirst action we take
Carel E2 / E3High or low temperature alarm — cabinet drifting out of rangeCheck probe position, fan operation and condenser airflow before touching gas
Carel E4 / E5Defrost or probe fault — ice build-up on the evaporatorForce a defrost cycle, test heaters, verify the termination probe
Dixell XR06 / XR03Parameter or sensor error after a power cutRe-read sensors against a calibrated thermometer, restore set points
Eliwell IDPlus AL1/AL2Generic alarm logged overnightPull the alarm history — it usually records the hour the plant started failing

Low refrigerant after a pinhole leak is the single most common root cause we find in for restaurants, server rooms and retail across Islington, Camden, Enfield, Barnet and Haringey. We pressure-test with trace gas, repair the leak, vacuum the circuit and recharge by weight — a legal F-Gas process, not a top-up. Compressor electricals get a full check: start capacitor, run current against the data plate, and winding resistance. If the compressor is tight or the valves are passing, we quote a replacement with the recovery and reclamation paperwork your EHO inspection expects.
