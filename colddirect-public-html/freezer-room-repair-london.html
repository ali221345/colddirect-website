Common Freezer Room Faults We Fix in London

  • Door frozen shut or dripping at the frame. A failed perimeter heater lets the gasket weld to the liner overnight. Staff then lever the door and tear the seal. We test the heater circuit and replace the gasket so the room can be opened without wrecking the leaf.
  • Ceiling coil iced after a missed defrost. If the clock or controller skips a cycle, frost blankets the evaporator and air stops moving. Product at the back of the room warms while the coil looks white. We restore the defrost sequence and check drain heat tape so melt does not refreeze in the tray.
  • Roof pack short-cycling on high pressure. Kitchen grease on a condenser, a seized fan or a blocked discharge grille makes the compressor trip. We clean or replace the fan, check the pressure switches, and only then look at gas. A noisy pack is often airflow, not a new compressor.
