Different Types of Ice on Commercial Ice Machines

Trade ice only · London commercial repair

Different types of ice in a London kitchen come from a commercial ice machine, not a domestic tray. Cube, gourmet, flake and nugget ice each use a different evaporator. When the bin is empty at Friday service, the fault is almost always the maker: scale on the plate, a failed pump, a dirty condenser or a water inlet that has stuck. Cube ice on catering ice machines

Full-dice and half-dice cubes are what most restaurants and hotels want in a glass. A cube machine freezes water on a vertical plate, then harvests with a hot-gas or harvest-valve cycle. Thin or cloudy cubes usually mean the water filter is spent or the inlet valve is not filling the trough. No harvest with a frozen plate is a harvest-valve, sensor or board fault. We repair Hoshizaki, Ice-O-Matic, Scotsman, Manitowoc and similar trade cube makers. Commercial only. We do not attend countertop home units.

Gourmet and large clear cubes

Gourmet or “crescent” ice takes longer on the evaporator. Bars like the slow melt. The same commercial refrigeration circuit still has to reject heat. A gourmet maker that ices the bin slowly on a hot pass is often a blocked condenser on the back of the machine, not a “wrong ice type”. Clear the grille before you assume the model is undersized. If the cycle time has doubled after a few months of hard water, descale is due and the pump may already be noisy.

Flake and nugget ice

Flake ice is for fish, produce and display. Nugget or chewable ice is for QSR and healthcare. Both use an auger. When the gearbox groans or the bin fills with wet slush, stop the machine. Running an auger dry wrecks the evaporator. That is commercial ice machine repair London work, not a weekend clean. Hoshizaki flake faults and Ice-O-Matic cube faults are booked on the same 24/7 numbers. See also Hoshizaki ice machine repair London.

What type of ice does not change the repair

Scale, dirty condensers, failed pumps and low water pressure look the same whether the bin is meant to hold cubes or flake. Probe the incoming water. Photograph the data plate. Do not chip ice off an evaporator with a knife. If the machine is in a hot kitchen next to a fryer, the condenser will load with grease the same way a commercial fridge does.
