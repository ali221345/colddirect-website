Common Display Fridge Faults We Fix in London

  • Self-closer worn so the glass door stays open. A tired cam or hinge lets the leaf rest a finger-width off the gasket. Shop air floods in, the evaporator ices, and the merchandiser looks “broken” by lunch. We fit the closer and a new gasket if the magnet has gone slack.
  • Cracked condenser fan blade on a plug-in unit. Vibration snaps a blade, the motor still hums, and head pressure climbs until the fridge cuts out. We replace the fan assembly and check the grille is not packed with till receipts and dust.
  • LED driver failed while the cabinet is still cold. Staff assume the fridge is dead because the door lights are out. We test the refrigeration circuit separately from the lighting transformer so you do not pay for a compressor you do not need.
