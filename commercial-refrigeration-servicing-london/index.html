Planned maintenance for London business refrigeration

Cold Direct provides planned commercial refrigeration maintenance for restaurants, pubs, hotels, shops and other trade sites. A planned visit is different from an emergency repair: it gives an engineer time to inspect the equipment while it is operating, discuss its condition and identify maintenance or repair needs.

The work depends on the equipment and its condition. A visit may include checks of condenser condition and airflow, door seals and closures, fans, controls and operating temperatures. Freezer checks may also include the defrost system and door heaters where fitted. The scope is agreed for the equipment at your site; a maintenance visit cannot prevent every breakdown.

Fridge service London for businesses

Planned commercial fridge servicing can cover upright, under-counter, preparation and display cabinets. Checks may include condenser airflow, door gaskets, fan operation, controls and temperature readings. Tell us the equipment type, make and model, how many units need attention and your postcode so we can discuss the right visit.

For a fridge that has already failed or is not holding temperature, use our commercial fridge repair London page for breakdown support.

Freezer service London for businesses

Commercial freezer servicing may include checks of condenser condition, door seals, fans, controls and temperature. Depending on the model, an engineer may also inspect defrost operation and door heaters. The checks are based on the freezer and the site rather than a one-size-fits-all checklist.

For a failed trade freezer or stock at risk, see commercial freezer repair London. Cold Direct works on commercial equipment; domestic appliance servicing is not offered.
