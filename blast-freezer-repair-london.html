Why Your Blast Freezer Stopped Working?

  • The core probe left hanging in the airstream so the controller thinks the batch is frozen and stops while chicken in the pan is still above minus ten.
  • A door microswitch stuck on open after a slam, so fans and compressor stay locked out while the display still looks live.
  • A three-phase inverter trips after a kitchen power dip; the cabinet sits silent until we reset and prove the programme against a probe in the food, not the air.

Why does the freeze cycle never finish?

A wet load, a probe not in the product, or a defrost that never completes will hold freeze until the coil is a brick. We check probe placement first. Many dead-compressor calls are a controller waiting for a temperature it will never see.

Can you repair three-phase blast cabinets in hotel kitchens?

Yes. Hotel and contract catering blast plant is often three-phase. Tell us the supply so we pack the right meter. We isolate inverter faults from a failed compressor so you do not pay for the wrong part.

Is a blast chiller the same job as a blast freezer?

No. A chiller pulls to plus three; a freezer must reach minus eighteen. The same team does both, but the programme, probes and gas charge differ. Book the chiller page if yours only chills; this page is freeze cycles that have stalled.
