Common faults we fix

  • Dirty air-cooled condenser: kitchen grease blankets the coil, head pressure climbs, the chiller trips on HP.
  • Blocked water strainer: water chillers starve the condenser, flow switches open, the pack locks out.
  • Failed condenser or evaporator fan: one dead motor ices a coil or overheats a compressor on commercial refrigeration plant.
  • Low refrigerant after a leak: we find the joint, repair it, pressure test, then charge. No gas-and-go.
  • Process pump or glycol mix: weak pump or wrong mix on a process chiller gives warm beer lines or a climbing bath.
  • Controller and probe faults: a drifted sensor makes a walk-in or display look fine on the screen while product is already warm.

Commercial Fridge Not Cooling / Leaking Water

Clearing blocked condensate drains, replacing seized evaporator fan motors, cleaning grease-choked condensers and recalibrating Dixell/Carel thermostats when cabinets climb above 5°C–8°C.

Walk-In Freezer Temperature High & Icing Up
